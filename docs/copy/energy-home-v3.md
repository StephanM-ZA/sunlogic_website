## 3 Why now

**H2** Your Bill Can Rise in a Month You Used Less

**Body** [v2]

Solar used to be about keeping the lights on. Not any more. We have gone well
over a year without load shedding, and people are installing today for one
reason. Electricity keeps getting more expensive.

At home that shows up as a bill you cannot plan around. In a business it shows
up in the cost of everything you make or sell, month after month, whether trade
was good or not.

**Callout** THE PART MOST PEOPLE MISS [v3, label restored]

It is not only the rate per unit going up. The fixed monthly charge has been
climbing faster than the rate.

> v2 narrowed this to THE PART MOST HOUSEHOLDS MISS so it could sit beside a
> business callout. With the business callout moved to solar.html, the label
> goes back to covering everyone, which it always did.

**Two different sums** [v2, kept]

Homes: most households use their power in the morning and the evening, when the
panels are not producing, so the sum turns on storage and on what you can shift.

Businesses: you trade during the day, which is exactly when solar produces, so
more of what you generate goes straight into the building.

**Close** [v2] We cannot change what your municipality charges. We can change
how much of it you pay.

**THE PART MOST BUSINESSES MISS** [MOVED to solar.html, business section]

On a commercial tariff you are billed on your highest demand of the month as
well as on what you used. One busy half hour sets a charge you carry all month.

> Detail, not recognition. It belongs where a reader who has already put their
> hand up can absorb it. Moving it also takes the one claim I cannot verify off
> the highest traffic page on the site.
