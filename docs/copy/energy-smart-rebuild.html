The page now argues something. Its weakest point is no longer the copy: it is that the two pillars you own carry no prices and no imagery, while the one you resell has exact figures and two product shots.

What was actually wrong

Not the writing. The scope. A page called Smart Solutions listed HotBot, SolarBot and a subscription, and stopped. Missing entirely: the thesis, smart switching, VoltIQ, smart home, the AI position, the grid-services honesty, and six FAQs. All of it was already written in your copy doc and none of it had been built.

Two of the three pillars are yours, and both were the missing ones. Switching is electrical work at the board, with nothing to subscribe to. VoltIQ you built and run. The page was giving all its space to the one thing you resell.

A whole section about something nobody can buy

grid services

The honesty is worth keeping. A full band of a long page is not, when the actionable content is one sentence: buy equipment that can join in later. Demoted to a callout inside the AI section.

The gate caught nine things in my own build

Worth recording, because it is the argument for having the gate at all. On first build this page failed with seven heading-case errors, an em dash inside a placeholder, and two adjacent bands sharing a background because a sector marker is not a band.

And it produced a new rule. Two grids on this page had one six-word heading in sentence case sitting beside four-word neighbours in Title Case. Rule 6 is right card by card and keeps generating sets that read as a slip. That had been fixed by hand on the energy home page, on solar, and twice here.

Rule 20Card headings inside one grid share one case. The defect is not either heading, it is the mix, so the mix is what gets measured. Proved by injecting a violation and watching the build fail.

Rule 20 also reports one grid on the Electrical site. That site is out of scope and stays untouched, so the gate carries one known failure there until Electrical is worked. It is a one-heading rewording whenever that happens.

Four things I cannot write without you

  • The VoltIQ subscription tiers. The page says "depending on your subscription", which is true and does no work at all. A reader choosing between tiers cannot choose from that. Needed: the tier names, what each includes, what each costs. It is sitting on the pillar that is entirely yours.
  • Prices for switching. Plentify's devices carry R849 and R149. Switching and monitoring carry nothing. That makes the product you resell look like the real offer and your own work look like an afterthought. Even "from R X, depending on your board" fixes it.
  • The smart home brand list. Currently a visible bracketed placeholder on the page, per the house rule that an unresolved fact is never silently omitted. It is the detail that decides who somebody phones.
  • The cancellation terms. Deliberately absent. The old page, the legal contract and Plentify's published terms give three different answers, and the contract uses "the Free Trial" as a capitalised defined term it never defines. Nothing goes on the page until those three agree.
