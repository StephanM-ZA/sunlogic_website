The Articles That Were Never Written

A confirmation pass on the Energy home page, then a sweep across the whole division. The home page came back close to finished. The sweep did not: the thank-you page had been advertising three articles for months, and two of them do not exist.

The home page is finished bar one referred item. The division around it was not, and none of what was wrong could have been caught by looking at a page on its own: every finding below came from comparing pages against each other, or against the files they claim to describe.

thank-you.html · Worth knowing

The page carried three article cards: Why Your Bill Went Up, Should You Get a Battery? and How to read a solar quote. Only the first corresponds to a published post. The other two were never written.

This is the failure mode the house rules exist to prevent, and it had been live the whole time. A gate that reads one page at a time cannot see it: the cards are well-formed, on-palette and correctly spaced. They are simply about nothing.

The rule caught its own trap

shared/sunlogic-check.js, rule 6

Canonicalising the titles made the gate fail on two blog posts, and the reason mattered. Rule 6 exempts article cards from heading case, because a post's title is not a label this system gets to set. That exemption keyed on the presence of a date. So a related-post card where nobody remembered the date silently stopped being an article card and started being judged as a label: the rule failed Why Your Electricity Bill Went Up on two pages while passing the identical heading on three others.

AppliedThe exemption now also accepts a card that links to a post, which is what actually makes it an article card.

Worth keeping in mind: an exemption keyed on an optional attribute quietly becomes a rule about whether somebody filled that attribute in.

Referred, not decided

  • The hero. "Every Unit You Make Is One You Don't Buy" is an economics claim and the body under it switches to method and scope. Both lines are good and provable, they just do not follow from each other. This is v3 copy you approved, so it stands until you say otherwise.
  • The load-shedding post is still promoted in the blog grid alongside the others. The argument moved off load shedding roughly eighteen months ago. Keeping it as archive is defensible; promoting it may not be.
  • Two of three blog cards are dated 2022. On a page dated 2026 that reads as a dead blog. A content decision, not a copy one.
  • Outside this division: site-electrical/thank-you.html carries the identical fabricated cards, including the battery article that does not exist. Left untouched on your instruction, flagged because it is the same invented content on a live page.

After this report was written

Three things changed on the home page after this sweep was filed. Recorded here so the report does not quietly go out of date, which is the same failure it criticises elsewhere.

  • Two of the three job cards were factually wrong in public. Noordhoek said "10kW of panels each" where it is a 10kWh battery per phase, and Rondebosch said 64kW of inverter capacity where it is six 12kW units, which is 72kW. Corrected from the owner's own figures, and all three cards now say what kind of property it was: a new residential development, a private residence on three phase, and Rondebosch Prep, named with permission.
  • The blog teaser was removed from the home page. Adding the dates made a four-year-old blog legible rather than causing it. Every presentational alternative was lipstick, and dropping the dates again would have been concealment. The markup and the condition for reinstating it (two or three posts from the current year) are preserved in a comment at that point in the file. The archive was never made unreachable.
  • The "one real number" promise was broken one line later by two cards that each gave three. Now "a photograph and what was actually fitted".

The load-shedding post stays, on the owner's decision: it is a real piece from when load shedding was a daily fact, and rewriting it would make it less true rather than more. One thing was added and not one word of the article was changed. It opens with "no end in sight to load shedding disruptions" and closes with a quote saying there is "no sign of load shedding easing any time soon". Both were accurate in July 2022 and neither is now, and although the post carries its byline date, a byline is not the same as telling a reader that the outlook in the first and last lines has since changed. An archive note now says so, above the article, carrying no duration figure: a number like "eighteen months without" needs maintaining and goes quietly wrong the moment nobody updates it.

Still open, and still the owner's: the hero, parked on instruction; and an SME proof point. The job cards are now two residences and a school, which is a real improvement on three anonymous suburbs, but the page argues to shops, offices, workshops and guesthouses and shows none of them. That needs a commercial job with a photograph, not copy.

Voice

The Energy home page went from nought contractions to eleven, so the division no longer reads as formal on the home page and spoken on solar one click away. Two formal constructions were kept on purpose: the statement panel's "We cannot change what your municipality charges. We can change how much of it you pay", where the cannot/can parallel is the whole device, and a card heading, because headings do not take contractions by default.

Reviewed against the Sunlogic house rules: no invented facts, no unverifiable claims, no jargon, no em dashes, no manufactured urgency, South African English.
