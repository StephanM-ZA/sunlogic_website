Why this one mattered more than its length suggested

The argument was already on the page

The whole home section turns on one idea: a unit you use yourself is worth considerably more than one you sell back. Everything else, the battery, the tariff warning, the morning and evening load profile, exists to serve that idea.

A car is the purest case of it there is, and for a reason nothing else on the page can match: its timing is entirely the owner's. A geyser has to fit around when people wash. A pool pump has a job to do. A car does not care whether it charges at two in the afternoon or two in the morning, only that it is full when they leave. That makes it the one load a reader can move into daylight without changing a single habit.

Your own copy document says as much, in the smart section: starting the geyser, the pool pump or the car charger when there is surplus is "the single most effective thing you can do with a solar system after installing it". That sentence never made it onto a page.
