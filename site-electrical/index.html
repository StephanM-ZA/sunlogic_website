The five that actually start the call

You’re selling. A transfer needs a Certificate of Compliance no older than two years, and the buyer's attorney will ask for it at the worst possible moment.

You’re claiming. Surge and fire claims turn on whether the installation was done properly and whether you can show it.

Your lease is up. The shopfit you inherited is normally yours to certify, and most people find that out at renewal.

You’re adding load. A car charger, air conditioning, an induction hob or solar. The board either carries it or it doesn't.

Something already broke. The circuit that keeps tripping, or the board that has had enough.
